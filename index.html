ABOUT

The person behind the work.

I want to understand why machines fly, burn and hold together, then help build the next one.

I am an aerospace engineering student at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, with the Foundation level of IIT Madras’s Data Science programme completed in 2024. My focus is propulsion: combustion, ignition, nozzles, and the high-speed flow that connects them.

This year it moved off the page. At Prime Toolings I designed and analysed a propulsion test rig in ANSYS and followed it through fabrication, welding and assembly, then worked on a hydrogen–oxygen torch igniter and static firing tests. At HAL I watched the LCA Tejas come together on final assembly. Alongside, I build sensing systems and design a tube-launched UAV.

How I work: fundamentals before shortcuts, the physics before the formula, theory tested against hardware, and code to make sense of the data.

Away from the desk: mountain trails, temple architecture, sketching and painting, and a camera. They are separate parts of my life, and I like keeping them that way.

Wireless Multi-Probe Capacitive Fuel Gauging with Dual-Regime Attitude Compensation

A wireless capacitive fuel gauging system using the FDC2214 capacitance-to-digital converter for liquid-level measurement. An MPU6050 IMU measures tank orientation for attitude compensation, and ESP32 boards communicate over ESP-NOW. A prototype was built and calibrated with a transparent rectangular tank and sunflower oil.
